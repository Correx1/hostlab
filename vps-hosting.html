Managed vs. unmanaged

Why pay for managed at all?

01

02

We carry the risk

Unpatched servers are the most common way sites get compromised. We patch on a schedule so you don't have to remember to.

03

Downtime gets a response, not a shrug

If something goes down at 2am, it's our alert to answer, not yours to discover the next morning.
